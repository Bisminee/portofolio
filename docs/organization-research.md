## PL FM

**Identity:** PL FM is listed as **UKM Radio Kampus Polinema (UKM PLFM)** among Politeknik Negeri Malang’s student organizations. Its public Instagram identifies it as **Radio Kampus Politeknik FM**.

**Relevant context:** As a campus radio organization, PL FM combines broadcasting with off-air activity. Creative Media supports visual content and social media, while Marketing and Public Relations handle promotion and external communication. The public account name and the organization listing corroborate the radio/creative-media context.

**Personal information supplied by Bisma:** Creative Media staff focused on Spotify and social media content (March 2025 – January 2026), then Steering Committee Manager Off Air (March 2026 – present). These roles and responsibilities are not inferred from the organization’s general structure.

**Research limitations:** Public sources confirm the organization and its radio identity. They do not independently document Bisma’s individual tasks or the internal Steering Committee structure. No internal PL FM documents are published.

## Polinema Mengajar

**Identity:** Polinema Mengajar is a student-run educational organization based in Malang. Its LinkedIn profile describes additional learning, skills training, and educational activities for children and young people, with Polinema students acting as facilitators and mentors.

**Relevant context:** The community’s focus is education and social contribution. Public recruitment material for the 2025/2026 period confirms an active student organization and a Media Information division structure, but it does not itemize every teaching location or activity.

**Personal information supplied by Bisma:** Graphic Design Staff in the Media Information (Medinfo) division, December 2025 – November 2026, producing non-video visual assets and participating in teaching at Rumah Belajar JKJT. Yasibu is also listed as a location in the supplied information.

**Research limitations:** The specific JKJT/Yasibu activities and Bisma’s teaching participation are user-provided and were not independently documented in the public sources found. The activity period is retained exactly as supplied; it is not marked completed before November 2026 ends.

## Robosarema

**Identity:** Robosarema is the robotics team/community at Politeknik Negeri Malang. The **KRI 2023 participant list** records “Politeknik Negeri Malang – ROBOSAREMA” in the KRSRI division. Its public GitHub organization maintains `sakin`, a C99 single-header 3-DOF leg IK/FK and gait engine for hexapods.

**Relevant context:** KRSRI is the Search and Rescue robot division of Indonesia’s national robotics competition. It requires autonomous legged robots, victim detection, obstacle traversal, and evacuation to a safety zone. Robosarema’s public library describes leg kinematics and ripple/amble/tripod gaits, consistent with that domain.

**Personal information supplied by Bisma:** Software Division member since December 2024, working with two other software members on kinematics, object detection, inter-module communication, and search-algorithm testing in C, C++, and Python.

**Research limitations:** The team’s main firmware repository is private, so its implementation is not cited or linked. Public sources verify the team identity and KRSRI context, not Bisma’s individual commits or responsibilities. The activity is therefore described as a team contribution, not a solo build.

## ROBOTIC UNLIMITED 2026

**Identity and organizer:** The official UNLIMITED 2026 site describes the KRSRI division as a national, offline Search and Rescue robot competition organized by Universitas Diponegoro (UNDIP). The event is for active D3/D4/S1 students, with teams of up to five.

**Venue and format:** Gedung Teknik Komputer UNDIP, Semarang; offline; autonomous legged robots with a gripper; no guide line; one start switch.

**Date discrepancy:** The official page’s timeline lists **18–19 September 2026**, while its “Hari Lomba” line lists **25 September 2026**. Bisma’s supplied participation date is **18 September 2026**. The portfolio uses the supplied participation date and does not claim a ranking, award, or final result.

**Research limitations:** The official page returned an intermittent server error during direct retrieval, so the content was verified from the indexed official page text. The date inconsistency is recorded here rather than resolved by assumption.

## Kangdjoe / Keju Artisan

**Research result:** No reliable public source was found that confirms the exact Kangdjoe identity, its ownership, or a documented rebrand to Keju Artisan. Search results for “Keju Artisan” returned unrelated cheese businesses (for example Kahf Artisan Cheese and Kejugja). Those were **not** used as sources because they are different entities.

**Personal information supplied by Bisma:** Instagram feed visuals, TikTok promotion, and product photography. These tasks are presented as user-provided experience, not independently verified public achievements.

**Research limitations:** Brand identity and dates remain unverified externally. No location or work format is displayed publicly.
